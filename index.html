About me

Hi, I'm Zach. I study computer science, with a minor in mathematics, at the University of Manitoba in Winnipeg. I'm interested in systems and algorithms, and in writing software that's clean enough to teach from.

This page has my projects and my resume. Email is the best way to reach me.

My projects

umPlan

TypeScript, 2026

An unofficial course planner for University of Manitoba students. It scrapes the public Academic Calendar (3,522 courses and 216 programs), parses every prerequisite, and checks your plan as you drag courses into terms. You can keep several plans side by side and compare them.

word2latex

Python, 2026

Turns photos of handwritten notes into structured LaTeX, running entirely on your own machine. It's built for theory and math notes: aligned derivations come out as align* and boxed results as theorem environments.

Farm-Clicker

TypeScript, 2026

An incremental clicker game for COMP 2452. Game logic is kept apart from the UI with MVC, and accounts are stored in PGlite so each player's farm is still there when they come back.

Library Management System

Java, 2025

A library manager for COMP 2450, driven from a REPL. It handles members, waitlists, account restrictions, reviews, and a floor plan for each library.
