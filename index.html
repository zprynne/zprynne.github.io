    My projects

    OpenLearn

    Python, 2026

    A study assistant for my own classes. It answers questions from the course materials I upload, using retrieval-augmented generation on a language model running locally on my Mac, with Claude or OpenAI as an optional fallback. It also turns course content into Anki flashcards and charts my review history.

    umPlan

    TypeScript, 2026

    An unofficial course planner for University of Manitoba students. It scrapes the public Academic Calendar (3,522 courses and 216 programs), parses every prerequisite, and checks your plan as you drag courses into terms. You can keep several plans side by side and compare them.

    Farm-Clicker

    TypeScript, 2026

    An incremental farming game in the browser, in the spirit of Cookie Clicker. Each player has an account with saved progress, stored in PGlite, and the game logic is kept separate from the UI.
