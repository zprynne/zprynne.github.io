Below are some projects I've worked on. You can also read my resume or email me.

My projects

UMGuesser

TypeScript, 2026

GeoGuessr for spots around the University of Manitoba campus. You see a photo or panorama, drop a pin on the campus map, and score up to 5,000 points based on distance. Ranked games feed a leaderboard; the server picks locations, enforces the timer and scores every guess so answers can't leak early.

umPlan

TypeScript, 2026

An unofficial course planner for University of Manitoba students. It scrapes the public Academic Calendar (3,522 courses and 216 programs), parses every prerequisite, and checks your plan as you drag courses into terms. You can keep several plans side by side and compare them.

Library Management System

Java, 2025

A library manager for COMP 2450, driven from a REPL. It handles members, waitlists, account restrictions, reviews, and a floor plan for each library.
